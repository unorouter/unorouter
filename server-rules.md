# UnoRouter server rules

Welcome to UnoRouter. This is the community for our AI gateway and chat client: one key for 200+ models, code agents, and character chat. Keep it friendly and on topic and everyone has a good time.

Staff have the final say on anything not spelled out here. Rules can change at any time, and staying in the server means you accept them.

## The basics

**1. Be respectful.** No harassment, hate speech, racism, sexism, or personal attacks. Disagree about models all you want, but keep it civil.

**2. Follow Discord's Terms of Service.** Anything that breaks Discord's rules breaks ours too.

**3. No spam.** No flooding channels, mass pings, wall-of-text dumps, or repeated messages.

**4. Keep it on topic.** Use channels for what they are for. Help questions in help, model talk in model channels, off topic in general.

**5. English first.** English keeps moderation workable. A few words in another language is fine, just do not run whole conversations staff cannot follow.

## Promotion

**6. No unsolicited self promotion or advertising.** Do not drop your own server, product, or referral link unless a channel is meant for it or staff give the go ahead. This includes advertising in DMs to members.

## Content

**7. Keep it clean.** This is a safe-for-work server for now. No NSFW images, text, or links. If there is real demand for NSFW spaces we may add gated channels later, but until then keep that content off the server.

**8. No illegal or harmful content.** No content sexualizing minors, no images of real people without consent, no piracy, no malware, no doxxing. This is an instant ban.

**9. No key or proxy begging.** Do not ask for free keys, leaked keys, or "working proxies," and do not share other people's keys or credentials. UnoRouter has free models built in, use those.

**10. Go easy on politics and religion.** They start fights that have nothing to do with AI. Keep it minimal.

## If something goes wrong

**11. Report, do not engage.** See a rule being broken or something that makes you uncomfortable? Do not fight it in chat. Message a staff member with a link and let them handle it.

We want this to be a welcoming place for people building with and using UnoRouter. Use common sense for anything not covered here.
